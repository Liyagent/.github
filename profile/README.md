<h1 align="center">Liyagent</h1>

<p align="center">Self-hosted AI agents that ask before they act.</p>

Liyagent runs AI agents inside the systems a company already uses — IT service desks, HR, SAP, Salesforce, Dynamics 365 and finance — and governs them: every agent acts as the person who asked, with that person's permissions; writes wait for a person's approval; spend is capped per agent; and every action lands in a hash-chained audit log whose exports are signed, so anyone can verify them.

It runs on your own infrastructure (a VM, Docker Compose or Kubernetes) with the model you choose, including open models on your own hardware.
